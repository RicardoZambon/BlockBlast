[Game name] is published by [Seller name] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have. Questions: [Support email].

In short

  • The game needs no account and never asks for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • The game is free because it shows ads. Our ad and analytics partners collect some information from your device, and only after you answer the consent request the game shows when you first open it.

What stays on your phone

Your game in progress, scores and best score, Daily Puzzle results and streak, your settings, and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved on your phone only. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What our partners collect

Advertising. AppLovin MAX (AppLovin Corporation) chooses which ad network shows each ad. The networks it currently uses for us are AppLovin, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Mintegral and Liftoff. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud they may collect:

  • a device identifier: the advertising ID on Android, and on iPhone and iPad the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as the model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ads you see.

Where you agree, they also use it to show ads that suit your interests. Their policies: AppLovin, Google, Unity, Mintegral, Liftoff.

Analytics, crash reports and settings. We use Google Firebase (Google LLC):

  • Analytics records how the game is played, for example when a game starts and ends, its score, and which ads were shown, together with an identifier for this installation and device information.
  • Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes, so we can fix it.
  • Remote Config delivers settings, such as how hard games are or how often ads appear.

We use this to improve the game. Firebase's policy: Firebase privacy.

Purchases. The App Store and Google Play handle payment for Remove Ads. We only learn that you own it, never your payment details.

Your choices

  • In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads and analytics. You can change your answer at any time in the game: Settings, then Privacy settings.
  • In US states with privacy laws, the game lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for targeted advertising.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks before tracking. You can change it later in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads).
  • Everywhere, you can turn off personalized ads in your phone's settings, and uninstalling the game deletes what it saved on your phone.

Children

The game is meant for people aged 13 and over. It isn't directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, write to us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the LGPD in Brazil or the CCPA in California), you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to its use. We hold none ourselves apart from emails you send us. For what our partners hold, write to us and we'll help you reach them, or use the tools their policies above describe.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is your consent for personalized ads and analytics, and otherwise our legitimate interest in running, protecting and improving a free game.
